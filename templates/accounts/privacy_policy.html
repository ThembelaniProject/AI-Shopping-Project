Privacy Policy

How SmartSpend handles your information

Last Updated: {{ last_updated|default:"20 September 2026" }}

1. Introduction

Welcome to SmartSpend. SmartSpend is a shopping recommendation application designed to help users discover products and make informed shopping decisions based on information provided by the user.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you when using the application.

2. Information We Collect

When you create and use a SmartSpend account, we may collect information such as:

  • Your first name and last name.
  • Your DUT student email address.
  • Your account login information.
  • Your shopping budget or available spending amount.
  • Shopping preferences and recommendation information.
  • Location information when you choose to provide it.
  • Information you provide while interacting with the application.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through SmartSpend may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate you when you log in.
  • Provide personalised shopping recommendations.
  • Use your selected budget when generating recommendations.
  • Provide location-related shopping functionality when enabled.
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of SmartSpend.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the application.

4. Location Information

SmartSpend may request access to your location to provide location-based shopping functionality.

Location access is optional. You can choose whether to allow your browser or device to provide your location.

When location information is provided, SmartSpend may use latitude and longitude coordinates to determine an approximate address or location relevant to the application's features.

5. Account Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with your SmartSpend account.

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and should not share your login credentials with other people.

6. Passwords

Your password is used to authenticate your account. Passwords should never be shared with other users.

SmartSpend does not intentionally display your password as readable text while it is stored or processed by the application's authentication system.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers where this is necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve SmartSpend, or where required by applicable law.

8. Third-Party Services

SmartSpend may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, such as maps, location processing, authentication, product information, or other application services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Cookies and Sessions

SmartSpend may use cookies and server-side sessions to maintain authentication, remember application settings, and provide functionality requested by the user.

10. Data Retention

Account information may be retained for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to provide the application's services.

Where appropriate, information may be deleted when it is no longer required for the purposes for which it was collected.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the functionality available in the application, you may be able to:

  • Update your name and email address.
  • Change your shopping budget.
  • Choose whether to provide location information.
  • Stop using the SmartSpend application.

12. Children's Privacy

SmartSpend is intended for users who are legally permitted to use the service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to SmartSpend, applicable requirements, or the way information is handled.

The updated version will be made available through the application.

14. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact the SmartSpend project administrators through the appropriate support channel provided by the application.
